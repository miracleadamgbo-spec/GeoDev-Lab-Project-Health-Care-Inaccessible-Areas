# My project brief
To identify and map wards in AMAC FCT-Abuja where residents have poor physical access to hospitals, in order to inform equitable healthcare planning and resource allocation.

## Spatial Question and Study Area
Which wards in FCT Abuja have populations living more than 3km or 15 minutes away from the nearest hospital
